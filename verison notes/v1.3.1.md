# Micforge v1.3.1 Release Notes

**Release Date:** November 2026  
**Status:** Low-Latency Overhaul, GIL Isolation & Naturalness • 134 Automated Tests Passing  

Micforge v1.3.1 is a fundamental performance and audio quality overhaul. It eliminates GIL contention by moving the real-time DSP engine into an independent process, slashes streaming latency by collapsing AEC, neural denoising, and dereverberation into a **single frequency-domain STFT pass**, eliminates the robotic "underwater" phase artifact with an in-domain **Naturalness dry/wet blend**, and introduces a **Free Neural Mask VAD** that drives gating, noise learning, and the Gamer HUD directly from speech probability.

## 🎛️ 2. Single Shared STFT Architecture (`src/micforge/dsp/spectral_rack.py`)

### The Challenge
In v1.3.0, the signal chain ran three consecutive frequency-domain processors:
1. `AcousticEchoCanceller` (PBFDAF + non-linear residual suppression)
2. `CoFiLiteDenoiser` (Neural STFT masking)
3. `RoomDereverberation` (Late reverberation power envelope suppression)

Each processor accumulated incoming time-domain samples, applied its own window, performed a forward FFT, applied spectral gains, performed an inverse FFT, and handled overlap-add (OLA) synthesis. This added **three cascading framing buffers**, 6 FFT/iFFT transforms per block, and cumulative phase distortion.

### The Solution
`SpectralRack` adapts AEC and De-Verb to CoFi-Lite's fixed 512-point STFT grid ($K = 257$ bins, $\text{hop} = 256$ samples):
1. **Single Analysis:** A single forward windowed FFT converts input audio into 257 complex frequency bins.
2. **Unified Frequency-Domain Pass:**
   - **Step A (AEC):** Linear echo estimate $\hat{D} = \sum W_p X_p$ is subtracted, and residual post-filter suppression is applied in the frequency domain.
   - **Step B (Denoise):** CoFi-Lite evaluates neural mask / complex speech spectrum, calculates Mask VAD, and applies the Naturalness blend.
   - **Step C (De-Verb):** Late reverberation power decay envelope tracks acoustic reflections and applies suppression gain directly to the spectrum.
3. **Single Synthesis:** A single inverse FFT and overlap-add synthesis produces the clean output.

**Benefits:**
- **Zero redundant transforms:** Slashes 4 FFT/iFFT calls per block.
- **Zero intermediate buffering:** De-Verb and AEC add zero additional framing latency to the chain.
- **Zero comb-filtering:** Eliminates phase smearing from multiple back-to-back overlap-add synthesizers.
- **Classical Wiener Fallback:** `SpectralDenoiser` operates on the exact same unified 257-bin grid when switched.

## 🌊 3. Dry/Wet Naturalness Blend (`naturalness: float = 0.08`)

### The Challenge
Aggressive neural speech enhancement often produces an artificial, hollow, or "underwater" phase artifact. Mixing time-domain raw audio back in after the chain causes comb-filtering because the raw microphone signal is not time-aligned with the denoiser's group delay.

### The Solution
- Micforge v1.3.1 introduces the **Naturalness** knob (default `0.08`, or 8% dry blend).
- **In-Domain STFT Mixing:** The dry microphone spectrum (`spec`) is blended back with the neural speech spectrum (`curr_enh`) **inside the frequency domain before the inverse FFT**:
  ```python
  if self.naturalness > 0.0:
      nat = min(0.30, max(0.0, self.naturalness))
      final_real = (1.0 - nat) * final_real + nat * spec.real
      final_imag = (1.0 - nat) * final_imag + nat * spec.imag
  ```
- Because dry and wet share the exact same STFT analysis frame, there is **zero delay mismatch** and **zero comb-filtering**. Subtle room air and acoustic micro-dynamics are restored, completely eliminating the underwater artifact.

## 🎙️ 4. Free Neural Mask VAD (Voice Activity Detection)

### The Challenge
Heuristic energy gates and RMS thresholds flutter during quiet speech or inter-word pauses, cutting off sentence endings and chattering on breathing sounds.

### The Solution
CoFi-Lite's neural magnitude mask $\mathcal{M} = |\hat{S}| / |Y|$ is inherently a high-resolution speech probability:
- **Voice-Band Averaging:** The mask is averaged across the core human vocal spectrum (300 Hz to 4,000 Hz, bins 10 to 128 at 16 kHz):
  $$\text{VAD}_{\text{raw}} = \frac{1}{118} \sum_{k=10}^{128} \mathcal{M}(k)$$
- **Attack/Release Smoothing:** Smoothed using fast attack ($\alpha_{\text{att}} = 0.75$) for instant speech onset detection and slow release ($\alpha_{\text{rel}} = 0.08$) to preserve word endings:
  ```python
  if raw_vad > self._smoothed_vad:
      self._smoothed_vad = 0.25 * self._smoothed_vad + 0.75 * raw_vad
  else:
      self._smoothed_vad = 0.92 * self._smoothed_vad + 0.08 * raw_vad
  ```
- **Hysteresis:** Engages voiced speech at $\ge 0.45$ probability and releases at $\le 0.25$ probability.
- **Unified Pipeline Driving:**
  - `NoiseGate`: Holds gate open during whispers and quiet consonants without chattering.
  - `AdaptiveNoiseProfiler`: Pauses adaptation during speech without false energy triggers.
  - `Smart Nap Mode`: Keeps neural model active during speech and engages 99% CPU savings during confirmed pauses.
  - `Gamer HUD`: Displays live neural speech confidence percentage: `[🎙️ VOICED 84%]`.

## 🛡️ 5. GIL Isolation & Worker Process Split (`src/micforge/audio/isolated_engine.py`)

### The Challenge
In Python, all threads share the Global Interpreter Lock (GIL). Rendering Rich terminal tables, handling Tkinter UI events, and processing Windows hotkeys competed with the real-time audio thread, causing intermittent audio clicks.

### The Solution
`IsolatedAudioEngine` moves the entire real-time streaming and DSP pipeline into an independent OS subprocess:
- **Audio Data via Shared Memory:** Waveforms flow through a dedicated `SharedAudioBuffer` using `multiprocessing.shared_memory`. Zero serialization, zero pickling, and zero IPC pipe buffer latency.
- **Instant Control Channel:** Mute, bypass, gain, and preset changes travel through a high-priority `multiprocessing.Queue` polled non-blockingly at block boundaries. Muting feels instantaneous ($< 1$ ms).
- **Shared Metrics Array:** Real-time levels, VU meter peaks, clipping flags, spectrum analyzer bands, and telemetry flow through an atomic shared array polled by the UI at 50 Hz with zero lock contention.
- **Automatic Crash Watchdog:** A background watchdog in the host process monitors the worker process. If the worker encounters an unexpected OS-level driver crash, the watchdog automatically revives the audio engine and restores active presets.
- **Windows Spawn & PyInstaller Ready:** Built from the ground up for Windows `spawn` semantics with `multiprocessing.freeze_support()` at application entry.
